Record-keeping approach

How entries are maintained

The diary records when decisions were made, how experiments were run and which files support the reported results.

01

Record plans first

Background searches, hypotheses and evaluation criteria are committed before experimental work starts.

02

Keep complete results

Entries include unsuccessful runs, unexpected behaviour and results that do not support the hypothesis.

03

Use stable references

Every entry has an identifier, dates, named investigators and a link to its project and core activity.
