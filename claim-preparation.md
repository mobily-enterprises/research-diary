## Do not submit diary entries as the application

The registration portal asks annual, activity-level questions. A diary entry records one experiment or iteration. At year end, group entries only where they genuinely address the same technical uncertainty, then prepare the portal answers from the complete evidence set.

## Public technical record

| Registration topic | Diary source |
| --- | --- |
| Project objectives and duration | Project page |
| Effective ownership, control and financial burden | Project page plus private contracts and approvals |
| Core activity description | Core-activity identifier and linked experiment series |
| Existing knowledge investigated | `Existing knowledge` sections and source records |
| Why the outcome was unknown | `Technical uncertainty` and `Competent professional assessment` |
| Hypothesis | Pre-experiment `Hypothesis` sections |
| Experiment | Protocols, variables, controls and work performed |
| Evaluation | Results, analysis method and comparison with thresholds |
| Logical conclusions | Conclusions and links to subsequent experiments |
| Evidence held | Entry evidence indexes and private record inventory |
| Supporting activities | Supporting entries and direct-relationship records |

## Private company record

Maintain these outside the public site:

- employment, payroll and superannuation records;
- contemporaneous time allocation by person and activity;
- general-ledger entries, invoices and proof of payment;
- cost apportionment calculations and adjustments;
- services agreements, statements of work and variations;
- IP ownership, licensing and open-source approvals;
- evidence of who directed the activities and bore failure risk;
- board or management approvals showing the purpose at commencement;
- adviser conclusions and the final registration and tax schedules.

Each private item should refer to the same stable project, core-activity and experiment identifiers used here.

## Current portal constraints

The published 2026 application guidance specifies character limits for activity descriptions and narrative answers. Several fields have a 4,000-character limit, and some core-activity responses have minimum lengths. Do not write diary entries to fit those limits. Prepare the annual answers from the underlying evidence and review the current form at lodgement time.

Registration remains self-assessment. Confirmation of registration is not a finding that activities or expenditure are eligible.

## Suggested year-end review

1. Freeze an evidence inventory for the income year.
2. Reassess whether each outcome was genuinely unknown in advance.
3. Separate core experiments, supporting work and routine development.
4. Confirm which entity owned, controlled, funded and benefited from the activities.
5. Reconcile time and expenditure to accounting records.
6. Group experiments only by the same technical uncertainty.
7. Draft portal responses and have the actual contracts and claim reviewed professionally.
8. Retain the complete technical and financial record for the required period.

Use the [current official application questions](https://business.gov.au/-/media/grants-and-programs/rdti/rdti-registration-application-form-questions-pdf.pdf) rather than treating this mapping as permanent.
