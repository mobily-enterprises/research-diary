## Supporting activity

Describe the scope, dates and main actions. Define a specific activity, not a department, job role or entire project.

## Related core activity

Identify every core R&D activity this work supported by stable identifier and name. A supporting activity cannot stand alone without a related core activity.

## Direct relationship

Explain how the activity directly supported the identified core activity. State which part of the experiment depended on this work.

## Work performed

Add material actions as they occur, with dates, names and links to retained records. Keep routine product delivery, administration and commercial work separate.

## Production or exclusion assessment

State whether the activity produced or was directly related to producing goods or services, or falls within a category excluded from being a core R&D activity. Record the facts supporting the assessment.

## Dominant purpose assessment

If the dominant-purpose test applies, record the company’s main purpose when the activity started, any other purposes, whether the work would have occurred without the core experiment, and why support for the core activity was the dominant purpose. If the test does not apply, give the reason.

## Records retained

List contemporaneous project plans, technical notes, communications, test materials, approvals, expenditure records and other evidence showing the direct relationship and, where applicable, dominant purpose.

## Conclusion

State what the work enabled and where the supporting activity ended. Do not invent a hypothesis for a supporting activity.
